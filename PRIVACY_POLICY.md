# MedHall Connect - Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** 2024

This Privacy Policy explains how MedHall Connect ("we," "us," "our," or the "Company") collects, uses, and protects your information.

---

## 1. INTRODUCTION

MedHall Connect is committed to protecting user privacy while maintaining necessary security and moderation controls.

**Key Principle:** Anonymous to other participants, but not invisible to MedHall's infrastructure.

This policy is transparent about what data we collect, how we use it, and who may access it.

---

## 2. WHAT INFORMATION WE COLLECT

### 2.1 Information You Provide

#### Registration Information
- Medical discipline or field
- Academic level
- Preferred language
- Country (optional)

We do NOT require:
- Real name
- Phone number
- Email address (unless you choose to provide it for support)
- Date of birth
- Address
- Government ID

#### Message Content
- Questions you ask
- Answers you provide
- Messages during sessions

#### Account Actions
- When you join or leave sessions
- Reports you file
- Users you block
- Time spent in sessions

### 2.2 Information from Telegram

MedHall receives from Telegram:
- Your Telegram user ID (for account linking)
- Your Telegram username (if applicable)
- Your Telegram display name
- Timestamp of messages

**What we do NOT have access to:**
- Your phone number (not exposed by Telegram Bot API)
- Your email address
- Your IP address (not directly)
- Other Telegram apps or chats
- Your contacts or groups

### 2.3 Technical Information

- Device type
- Operating system
- Bot interaction timestamps
- Message metadata
- Error logs related to your sessions

### 2.4 Derived Information

- Your reputation score (based on behavior)
- Your risk assessment (for abuse detection)
- Session history and statistics
- Matching frequency and success

## 4. WHO CAN ACCESS YOUR INFORMATION

### 4.1 Other Participants
**Messages:** The other participant in your session sees your message content but NOT:
- Your Telegram ID
- Your Telegram username
- Your real name
- Your location
- Your profile picture
- Any identifying metadata

**Participants use internal session IDs,** not your Telegram identifiers.

### 4.2 MedHall Employees & Authorized Personnel

#### Developers
- Can access conversation data for:
  - Debugging technical issues
  - Improving infrastructure
  - Security incident investigation
  - Product development

#### Moderators
- Can access conversation data for:
  - Investigating reports
  - Enforcing violations
  - Abuse prevention

#### Administrators
- Have broader access for:
  - System management
  - Policy enforcement
  - Legal compliance
  - Security incidents

**Access Controls:** Not all employees have access to all data. Role-based permissions are enforced.

### 4.3 Third Parties

#### Telegram
- Telegram Inc. receives all message traffic (as the communication medium)
- See Telegram's Privacy Policy for their practices

#### AI Providers
- If using external AI services, questions and responses are sent to those providers
- Current provider: Anthropic
- See their privacy policies at: https://www.anthropic.com/privacy

#### Database Host
- Cloud providers (if applicable) have infrastructure access
- Subject to data processing agreements

#### Law Enforcement
- Only upon lawful court order or legal process
- We will notify you unless legally prohibited

### 4.4 What Triggers Third-Party Access
- Legal request (court order, subpoena)
- Emergency/safety situation
- Necessary for AI fallback (anonymized)
- Data processing compliance

## 6. DATA RETENTION & DELETION

### 6.1 Retention Policy

| Data Type | Retention Duration | Reason |
|-----------|-------------------|--------|
| Active session messages | 90 days after session end | Legal compliance, investigation |
| Session metadata | 90 days | Audit trail |
| Reports | 730 days (2 years) | Legal requirement, pattern detection |
| Moderation actions | 730 days | Enforce repeat-violation policies |
| User profile | Until deletion request | Account continuity |
| Deleted account data | 30 days | Data subject rights |

### 6.2 Data Deletion
- You may request deletion of your account
- Associated messages older than 90 days are deleted
- Recent messages may be retained for investigation
- Deletion does not apply to legal holds or active investigations

### 6.3 Automatic Cleanup
- System performs automatic deletion of expired data
- Happens regularly without manual intervention

## 7. SECURITY MEASURES

### 7.1 Technical Security
- Encrypted database connections (TLS 1.2+)
- Secrets stored securely (not in code/logs)
- Rate limiting against abuse
- Content moderation filtering
- Session isolation (one user can't access another's session)

### 7.2 Access Control
- Role-based permissions
- Multi-factor authentication for admin access
- Audit logging of data access
- Session timeout for inactive sessions

### 7.3 Monitoring
- Intrusion detection
- Abuse pattern monitoring
- Automated alerting

### 7.4 Limitations
No system is 100% secure. While we implement strong security:
- Sophisticated attacks may succeed
- System breaches are possible
- Insider threats are a risk
- We cannot guarantee absolute security
